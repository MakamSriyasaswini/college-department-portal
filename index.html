About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering provides quality education in computing, software development, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. The department focuses on academic excellence, practical learning, research, and innovation.

Latest Department Update

The department has introduced new cloud computing and artificial intelligence facilities for the 2026 academic year. Students can now participate in additional hands-on laboratory sessions and technical workshops.
